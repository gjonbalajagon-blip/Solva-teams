How we work

One team, built with you and run end to end.

Four steps from first call to a live team. You stay in control of who joins it.

1

Understand

You tell us the role: language level, hours, volume, and what “good” looks like for your customers.

2

Build

We recruit and test in Prishtina, then set up the workspace, tools, backup power and redundant internet.

3

Select together

We select for language, capability and fit, then agree the team structure with you. We bring you a shortlist of strong candidates for the final interview, and you make the final choice.

You decide who joins your team
4

Run together

The team goes live, billed monthly per active full-time agent. We handle the people and the risk; leadership stays involved on every account.

Retention is the product.

In this business, the teams that stay are the ones that get good. We employ the agents and build a place worth staying in, so you’re not paying to re-train a new team every quarter.
